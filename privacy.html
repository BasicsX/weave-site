Weave Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Weave is a note-taking and knowledge-graph app. This policy explains what happens to your information. The short version: Weave has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers of its own. Your notes stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

What Weave stores

Notes, tasks, projects, people, tags and the links between them. This data is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, it is also synced between your iPhone, iPad and Mac through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit, private database), which only you can access. The developer of Weave cannot see it.

What Weave does not do

  • It does not collect analytics, usage data or crash data.
  • It does not track you or use advertising identifiers.
  • It does not sell or share your data.
  • It does not require an account.

Optional features that send data elsewhere

These are off until you turn them on.

AI answers with Claude (Anthropic). If you add your own Claude API key in Settings, then when you ask a question in the Ask tab, the question and the few most relevant notes are sent to Anthropic to generate an answer. Your key is stored in your device's Keychain. Anthropic's handling of that data is described in its own privacy policy (anthropic.com/privacy). Search itself always runs on your device. If you use Apple Intelligence on a supported device, answers are generated on-device.

Connected accounts (Outlook, Teams, Gmail, Slack, GitHub). If you connect one, Weave signs in through the provider's own login page and reads your mail, messages, or issues (read-only) directly from the provider to your device. Access tokens are stored in your Keychain. Data goes straight between your device and the provider; it does not pass through any server operated by the developer. You can disconnect at any time in the Connect tab, and remove the imported items.

Calendar and Reminders. If you allow access, Weave reads events and reminders on your device using Apple's system permission. Nothing leaves your device through this feature, apart from normal iCloud sync of the notes it creates.

Sharing, widgets and shortcuts

The Share extension, home-screen widget and Siri/Shortcuts actions exchange data with the app only on your device, through a shared app container.

Exporting

"Export to Markdown" creates a file on your device that you choose where to save or share.

Deleting your data

Delete notes in the app, or use Settings to erase all data. With iCloud sync on, deletions sync to your other devices. You can also remove Weave's iCloud data in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Children

Weave is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
