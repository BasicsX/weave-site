Weave Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email weavenotes@icloud.com and include your device, iOS or macOS version, and what happened.

Common questions

My notes aren't syncing. Make sure every device is signed in to the same Apple Account and iCloud is on (Settings > [your name] > iCloud). A red badge on the Settings tab means iCloud is signed out. Syncing can take a minute.

Where is my data? On your device and in your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.

How do I get my notes out? Settings > Export to Markdown creates a folder of .md files you can open in any notes app.

How do I use AI answers? Add your own Claude API key in Settings, or use Apple Intelligence on supported devices.

Can I connect iMessage, WhatsApp or Signal? No. iOS doesn't let apps read those conversations.
